# Solve cases

- Standalone complete-answer request: solve directly without mandatory course intake or prior attempt.
- Essential missing given: identify it; do not fabricate a determinate answer.
- Source answer exists: verify it and investigate conflicts with independent reasoning.
- No official answer: label agent derivation and use meaningful independent checks.
- Active drill collision: provide the requested full answer and save changed exposure; do not grant mastery.
- Explicit solution-only request: finish without issuing another exercise.
- Ongoing study continuation: use a fresh verified transfer task when appropriate.
- Multiline equations followed by prose and headings: saved display delimiters each occupy their own line; render the complete Markdown with math support and verify no KaTeX errors or prose swallowed into formulas. Include multiple adjacent derivations, not just isolated expressions.

Primary example solutions are Markdown; there is no synchronized JSON artifact.
