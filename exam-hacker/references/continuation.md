# Continue to the next learner action

Apply this from every specialist, including direct invocation. Read only the next specialist's instructions when its operation becomes necessary. The shared entry is `exam-hacker/references/learning-record.md`.

## Decision rules

1. Honor the requested scope first. For “only solve/explain/save/stop”, complete that request; do not force a diagnostic, study plan, or additional exercise.
2. Reuse existing observations and pending work. A learning state belongs to a capability under particular conditions, not to a permanent type of learner.
3. If a missing distinction would change the next action, use the smallest useful probe or question. Do not put a mandatory diagnostic before every block. A user's explicit preference to learn first or practice first takes precedence.
4. For a known missing framework or local misconception, use compress; for demonstrated skill, practice transfer; for insufficient time or changed priorities, use replan.
5. Continue internal work until one real learner action is ready. Present that action immediately, then wait. Never invent the learner's response.
6. If no internal step would change the action, stop dispatching skills. No round-trip through the router is required.

## End of turn

Give a short rationale only where it helps, a concise change receipt when state changed, then one executable task:
- what to do, with the actual question or a precise file/section link;
- an effort estimate consistent with known capacity;
- what the user should submit;
- the observable pass condition, without leaking the solution.

Do not end with just “invoke replan/drill”, a menu of equally weighted options, or “shall I continue?” when the next step is known. If a user decision is necessary, give the concrete tradeoff. Do not require a reply to approve records or normal skill transitions.

Example after observing a compatibility gap: briefly name the gap and its evidence; supply the minimum corrective explanation; present a new short compatibility task and ask for its equation and boundary condition. Merely announcing that such a task will be supplied is not completion.

## Natural limits

Pause at a pending answer, a missing essential input, a conflicting goal needing user choice, or an explicit user stop. Do not automatically continue through an entire chapter. Do not require a new chat; offer a saved restart sentence when it is useful.

Artifacts are preparation, not performance. Reading a note, seeing a full solution, or saying “understood” does not justify skipping a topic as mastered. Save such feedback accurately and select a fresh task when verification matters.
